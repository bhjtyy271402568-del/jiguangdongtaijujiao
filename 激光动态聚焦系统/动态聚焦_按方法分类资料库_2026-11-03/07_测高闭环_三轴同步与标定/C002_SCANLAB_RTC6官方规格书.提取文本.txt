RTC 6
RTC6 control boards enable smart and flexible control of scan 
systems, lasers and peripheral devices in real time. 
Key Features:
• SCANahead control 
• UltraFastPixelMode (up to 3.2 MHz)
• Enlarged list memory
• Standalone functonality for RTC6 Ethernet
Based on the field-proven RTC5, the RTC6 control board delivers 
new functionalities to tackle complex control tasks. Examples are 
improved speed and position-dependent laser control and the 
synchronization of the usually faster x/y scan axes with the slower 
z-axis. This allows more precise results in 3D processing.
Complete incorporation of all RTC5 functions ensures quick, easy 
migration to the new RTC6 board.
