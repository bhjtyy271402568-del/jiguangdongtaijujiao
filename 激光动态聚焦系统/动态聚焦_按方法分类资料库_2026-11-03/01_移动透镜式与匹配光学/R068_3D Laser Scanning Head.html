3D Laser Scanning Head

A 3D laser scanning head is a laser beam control device designed for three-dimensional laser processing. It enables the laser beam to follow different surface contours and maintain precise processing across varying focal positions, making it suitable for curved and uneven workpieces as well as large-format applications.


Scanner Optics' 3D Laser Scanning Head combines high-performance galvo motors with a dynamic axis for fast and precise scanning over three-dimensional surfaces and large working areas. It is widely used in high-end laser processing applications, including large-format precision laser marking, laser engraving, deep laser engraving, laser cutting, and laser welding.

GET A QUOTE

Key Features of  3D Laser Scanning Head


  • Combines galvo motors and voice coil motors to achieve fast scanning of three-dimensional surfaces and large working areas.


  • Uses the TCP/IP communication protocol for computer communication, allowing one computer to control multiple 3D galvo scanning systems simultaneously.


  • The optical path is designed to optimize laser focusing performance across different scanning areas, ensuring a minimum laser spot size and more concentrated energy within different working ranges.


  • Highly integrated system design for easy installation and operation.
