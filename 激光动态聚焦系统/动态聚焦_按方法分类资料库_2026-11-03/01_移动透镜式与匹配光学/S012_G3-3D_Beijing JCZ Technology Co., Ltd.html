Product Information
Typical Application

Main Configurations

  • Marking Speed：4000mm/s
  • Positioning Speed：10000mm/s
  • Tracking Error：0.25ms
  • Non-Linearity：<3.5mrad/44°
  • Time for 1% ：≤0.4ms
  • Gain Drift：<50PPM/k
  • Offset Drift：<15μrad/k
  • Drift Over 8 Hours：<0.3mrad
  • Operating Deflection Angle：±0.35rad
  • Lens Spot：10mm
  • Surface：XY2-100
  • Power Supply：±15VDC, 3A
  • Operating Temperature：25℃±10℃
  • Storage Temperature：-10℃~+60℃
  • Marking Field：150(F-theta Lens Changeable)
  • Beam Expansion Ratio：1.4/3
  • Wavelength：1064nm, 532nm, 355nm
  • Input Aperture：7mm、7mm、3mm
  • Focal Range：±30mm(for F254-174), ±8mm(for F100-60), ±15mm(for F254-175)

Applications

It can be applied for mold processing, curved surface marking, surface treatment, deep engraving, etc.
