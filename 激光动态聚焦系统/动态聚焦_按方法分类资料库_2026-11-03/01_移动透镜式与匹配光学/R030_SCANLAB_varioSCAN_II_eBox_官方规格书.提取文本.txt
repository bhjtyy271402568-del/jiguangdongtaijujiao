varioSCAN II z-axes shift the laser focus 
along the optical axis. This is done by 
the moving optics, which expands the 
incoming laser beam. Then the laser beam 
is either collimated or focused by the fixed 
optics.
This results in two optics configurations: 
Type FT (F-Theta) or Type PR (PRefocus). 
Therefore the varioSCAN II can be used in 
systems configurations with or without an 
F-Theta objective.
