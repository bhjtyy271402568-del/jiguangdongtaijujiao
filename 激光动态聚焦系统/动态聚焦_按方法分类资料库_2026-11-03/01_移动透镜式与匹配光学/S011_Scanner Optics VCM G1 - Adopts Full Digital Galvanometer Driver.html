VCM G1

VCM G1 adopts full digital galvanometer driver, digital driver board of basic FPGA, stable performance, not easy to be disturbed, flexible to use, easy to debug. Modular design, easy to assemble. According to customer requirements, we can choose lenses with different aperture, high positioning accuracy and small drift. Mainly used for dynamic focusing, can be combined with the laser scanner head to form 3D modules, widely used in 3D marking, 3D printing, large area processing and other laser marking applications.

GET A QUOTE

Key Features Of VCM G1


  • The VCM G1 adopts a new mechanical design that cleverly converts the rotary motion of the galvanometer into linear motion


  • The dynamic following error is less than 1.5ms, and the response speed is improved by more than 50% compared to traditional voice coil motors


  • All-digital motor drive scheme, stable performance
